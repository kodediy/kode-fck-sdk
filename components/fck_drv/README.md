fck_drv
=======

The drivers of the module, one folder per part: the line protocol of the two firmwares, the C5 and its ROM loader, the nRF and its MCUboot, the CC1101, the GNSS sentences, the expander, the UARTs and the pins. Only the owner calls a driver.

Depends on: fck_bsp, kdefs from kode-sdk, and espressif/esp-serial-flasher

Docs: https://docs.kode.diy/modules/fck
